Arrow Expert: Privacy Policy

Sussal Productions

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

Arrow Expert itself does not collect, send or share any personal data: your progress stays on your device. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising ID and some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below. The web version shows no ads.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress (levels, coins, diamonds, hints, achievements, items, settings and the name you type) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage.
  • It is never sent to us. There is no server of ours that receives it.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this progress for good.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows full-screen ads between some levels and optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a hint, a heart, coins, a diamond or double coins). These ads come from Google AdMob.

  • Google may collect and use the advertising ID, your IP address, device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form asks you first. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins and diamonds are earned by playing (and some rewards by watching an ad).
  • No analytics or tracking of our own; the only third party is Google AdMob, for ads.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No chat and nothing you make is shown to other people.

Children

Arrow Expert does not itself collect any data from anyone, children included. Ads are shown by Google AdMob as described above.

Changes

If a future version changes what is described here, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
