Arrow Expert: Privacy Policy

Sussal Productions

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

Arrow Expert does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress (levels, coins, diamonds, hints, achievements, items, settings and the name you type) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage.
  • It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server that receives it.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this progress for good.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins and diamonds are earned only by playing.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No chat and nothing you make is shown to other people.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not collect any data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version changes what is described here (for example by adding ads or purchases), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
