## App name

Arrow Expert

## Short description

Tap arrows in the right order to clear the board. Calm, clever logic puzzles.

## Full description

Every arrow wants out. Tap one and it slides off the board, but only if nothing stands in front of it. Tap the wrong one and it bumps, and you lose a heart. Find the right order and clear the whole board.

Simple to learn, tricky to master
Each level is a tangle of arrows pointing every way. Some are free right away, most are blocked by others. Look ahead, plan a few moves, and watch the board open up one arrow at a time.

Endless levels
New boards are made for every level, from small warm-ups to big, dense puzzles. Difficulty rises slowly through Normal, Hard, Super Hard and Expert, so there is always a next challenge.

450+ shape puzzles
Every third level is a shape: letters, numbers, animals, food, objects and more, drawn with arrows. Clear the shape to finish the level.

Snake arrows
Long arrows bend and wrap around each other in lanes, so one tap can open a whole path, or block it.

Weekly quests and daily rewards
Six new quests every week, a points bar with three reward chests, and a daily reward that grows with your streak.

Achievements, badges and trophies
Claim achievements as you play, collect award badges and fill a trophy for every month.

Make it yours
Earn coins and diamonds by playing, then unlock arrow styles, board backgrounds, escape effects, confetti, sound packs, avatars and frames.

Play your way
Light and dark themes, pinch to zoom on big boards, hints when you are stuck, and full offline play.

No ads and no in-app purchases. Everything is earned by playing.
